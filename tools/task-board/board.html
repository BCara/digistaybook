How shared saving works

In Chrome or Edge, click Connect NAS task file and choose DigiStayBook-tasks.json from this NAS folder. Allow editing when prompted. Changes then save automatically to that file. The other person connects the same file to see the same board. The board checks for changes every 10 seconds and pauses on a detected conflict; edit in turns when working together.

Until connected, changes are a draft in this browser only. Download a backup before switching browsers or computers. Browsers without file-editing support can use import/download; they cannot automatically save to the NAS. Assigning a person records an owner here and does not send them a notification.

Milestone dates

Set a date to schedule its tasks. Moving it recalculates automatic targets using weekdays, without public-holiday adjustments. Tasks with an explicit date keep that date.

Scheduling assumptions

Initial groups and lead times are suggested planning defaults. Dates stay unset until you choose them. Legal preparation usually sits 10 weekdays before legal review; engineering preparation 5–10 weekdays before the controlled test; launch preparation 5 weekdays before public launch. You can change each task’s milestone, lead time or fixed target.
