Nineteen of 20 main posts have a per-item tracked destination; one is deliberately for useful replies. Practical posts offer a complete tip in the image, then an example in the demo. Remove weak or misleading hooks

Active copy drops “Guests don’t trash homes, they trash listings”, unsupported claims that a welcome changes behaviour, invented first-person host biographies, findings that have not been collected, and platform-policy assertions. “Control your own narrative” means the host’s own welcome and DigiStayBook wall. Booking-platform reviews remain there. Policy-sensitive source notes are archived research context, not verified platform advice.

Live Analytics account discovery failed because application-default credentials were missing. No reach, click, signup or conversion baseline was retrieved. Cadence and creative choices are hypotheses to test; traffic improvement is not yet measured.
