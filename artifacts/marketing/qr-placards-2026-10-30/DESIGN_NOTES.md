# DigiStayBook QR placard concepts

Created October 30, 2026. Draft design exploration only; not added to the active campaign or published.

Four original code-native designs: warm terracotta arch; botanical sage; coastal blue; editorial black and cream. Rendered with Sharp from editable SVG. No image-generation tool used. Existing logo: marketing/reference/digistaybook-logo-long-transparent.png. No photography or customer claims. Copy supports the product's house guidance, local favorites, and guest memories.

Each pin is 1000 × 1500 px. Print artwork is A5 (148 × 210 mm), plus 1748 × 2480 px PNG with 300 dpi metadata. For home/office printing; no bleed or crop marks.

All codes encode https://digistaybook.com/stay/demo-cottage, the fictional demo property; four-module white quiet zone, M error correction. Replace BOTH property name and QR destination with the actual in-stay link (including its access token where needed) for a host's sign. These are concept drafts rather than a claim that the product already offers these templates. Physical print/device scanning remains to be checked.
