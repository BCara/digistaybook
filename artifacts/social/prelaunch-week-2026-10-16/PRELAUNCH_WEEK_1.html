How DigiStayBook works, in three steps:

1️⃣ You set up your property's page once: welcome note, house guidance, local tips.
2️⃣ You print the QR display and pop it on the kitchen counter.
3️⃣ Guests scan it with their phone camera. They read everything they need, then leave a message or photo on your guest wall before they go.

Caption

Two questions hosts often ask about digital guestbooks:

"My guests won't download an app."
They don't have to. DigiStayBook opens in the phone's browser straight from the QR code. No app, no account, no password.

"What if someone posts something I don't want there?"
You're in control. Every new memory goes through automatic safety checks, you can turn on approval so nothing appears until you say so, and you can hide or remove any post from your dashboard.

What else would you want to know before using a digital guestbook? Ask away below.
