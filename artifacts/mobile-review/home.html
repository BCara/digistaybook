How it works

From a printed QR display to a wall full of memories.

  1. Put the QR display out

    One QR kit per property. It goes on the counter, in the welcome folder, or by the door.

  2. Guests scan on arrival

    The wall opens in the browser. Wi-Fi, bin day and your local favourites are pinned to the top.

  3. Notes arrive, screened

    Photos and messages pass automated screening before they publish. Anything borderline waits for you.

What hosts get

A guestbook, a house manual and a moderation queue.

Everything below is in both plans. There is no feature tier to decode.

01A wall guests actually use

A mobile-first run of photos and notes from every stay. No account, no download, nothing in the way.

02Your house manual, pinned

Wi-Fi, bin days, appliance quirks, checkout steps and recommendations sit above the wall, so the questions stop arriving.

03Screened before it publishes

Uploads land in private storage and pass image and text screening first. Severe cases never reach your inbox.

04One dashboard, every property

Curate, pin, hide and delete across all your properties, with clear active and unpaid states.

05QR kit per property

Print, download, email or copy the link. Live routing unlocks the moment a property is activated.

06Privacy designed in

Consent capture, self-service deletion, a report flag on every post, and a public Privacy & Safety route that does not depend on your inbox.

Why it matters

A stay that feels considered is a stay people talk about.

Create a more memorable guest experience—one that may encourage guests to share more positive feedback on their official booking platform.

DigiStayBook never asks guests for a rating, never pre-fills a review and never links into a booking provider’s review form. Improved feedback is a possible indirect benefit of a better stay, not a guaranteed outcome.
