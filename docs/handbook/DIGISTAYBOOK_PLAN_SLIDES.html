Part one

The business

Executive summary, objective, product, customer, roles and scope.

DigiStayBook sits between a host and their guests for the duration of a stay. It does not sit between a host and their booking provider, and it does not take on the guest relationship.

A digital guestbook, sold per property

A host creates a property, seeds it with a welcome post and house information, and receives a printed QR placard for the kitchen counter. Guests scan it during their stay, read the pinned house information, and add photos and messages to a shared wall — no app, no account, no password.

2.2 · Customer / who pays and who does not

The host pays. The guest is never asked to.

The host — the paying customer

An individual owner with one property, or a small operator running several. They already use Airbnb, Vrbo or Booking.com for reservations, payments, messaging and reviews.

Their problem is not distribution. It is that the stay itself has become impersonal, that house information is fragmented, and that the same questions arrive by message for every booking.

They have very little tolerance for another dashboard to check, and none at all for a product that generates support work.

The guest — never the customer

In an unfamiliar house for a few nights, holding a phone, with no reason to install anything.

The constraint that follows: the product must be usable by a guest who has never heard of DigiStayBook, in under a minute, on a phone with poor signal, without registering.

3.1 · Customer problem / why a host would care

Efficient and impersonal at the same time

Self check-in, keypad locks and automated messaging mean a guest can arrive, stay and leave without a single human exchange. The stay works, but it feels like a transaction in an empty house.

The stay has no character

Nothing in the property tells the guest that someone cares about it, and nothing invites the guest to treat it as more than a room they paid for.

Information arrives at the wrong time

Wi-Fi passwords, bin nights, appliance quirks and checkout steps are spread across the booking thread, a folder nobody opens, and a welcome message read three weeks before arrival. The same questions then arrive by message, per booking, forever.

Nothing accumulates

Every stay starts from zero. A guestbook on the hall table does accumulate — but only the next guest at that table can see it, and it cannot be moderated, backed up or removed on request.

The impact: a stay that is competent but forgettable, and a support load that never falls. Hosts care because guest experience is the one lever they control that a booking provider does not set for them.

No interviews, survey responses or waitlist data exist. Decide whether to run even a small structured conversation with ten hosts before launch spend is committed.

4.3 · Commercial principles / nine rules that bind every surface

Commercial principles

One offer, one wording, one price. Marketing, the pricing page, checkout, dashboard entitlements and the customer policies describe the same offer, in the same words, at the same price.

Limits are visible before they are reached, and the exact reason is shown when an action is blocked.

Cancellation is self-service in the dashboard and nowhere else. Requests are not accepted by email or contact form, because neither route can verify account ownership.

On cancellation the host keeps full access to the wall and QR code until the end of the paid cycle or trial, and is told the exact date access ends and what happens to the content afterwards.

Charges are non-refundable except where required by law, including a major failure under the Australian Consumer Law. Statutory rights are never described as a goodwill concession.

Goodwill refunds outside the policy require a recorded decision by a named owner and are reconciled against Stripe.

No promise that has not been implemented, costed and substantiated. Not unlimited storage, indefinite retention, original-quality media, guaranteed uptime, or any review, revenue or property outcome.

A failed payment never produces a broken link for a guest standing in the property. It produces a designed fallback page. Protecting the host's appearance in front of their own guests is a commercial rule, not a courtesy.

Nothing reaches a public wall unscreened

On submission, files upload to a private, non-public bucket and the post is assigned a processing status. While the guest sees a brief optimistic loading state, a dual-layer server-side scan runs: image moderation and concurrent text filtering — string matching for profanity, sentiment analysis and URL detection.

Fail-closed

If the moderation provider is unavailable, new content stays pending and does not appear publicly. It is never published optimistically and reviewed later.

No biometrics — a hard constraint

No biometric data and no facial-recognition template is generated, extracted or stored from a guest image. Driven by biometric privacy law, not preference.

Why the pending state matters

Without it, a guest caught by a false positive assumes the app is broken and uploads the same images repeatedly.

Illegal or clearly dangerous materialLock down immediately, route to internal Trust & Safety, follow the documented incident and legal process. Mandatory authority reporting requires manual human verification — never an automated report.

The daily sweep

Operations reviews the critical escalation queue once per day. That queue holds only AI-flagged severe cases and host-initiated escalations — never ordinary curation. Each case is resolved by restricting content and session where proportionate, retaining a restricted case only for the approved review period, deleting securely when review and legal-retention needs end, or downgrading a false positive to host review.

Monthly tuning

A random sample of 50 standard AI-flagged posts each month. Over-aggressive filtering creates guest friction, so where the false-positive rate exceeds the accepted margin, the image-moderation confidence threshold is lowered. Unresolved critical cases are reviewed at least every 30 days.

Audit without copying media

Every moderation action records the previous status, the new status, the actor, the time and the reason — without copying the reported media into the audit event. Hosts are notified immediately when a post is hidden pending review; other notices are batched rather than one email per held item.

One transactional server endpoint governs visibility

Client-side actions and direct database writes can never hide, restore or delete another guest's post.

StateCauseEffect

Nothing is silently dropped

Every syntactically valid report receives the same neutral acknowledgement — "Report received. It will be reviewed." The client never claims deletion was approved and never reveals whether visibility changed. Reports received during a circuit-breaker state are logged, acknowledged, routed for internal review and left visible.

Report ID, post and wall IDs, pseudonymous reporter key, reason, App Check result, threshold counters, previous and new visibility, status, timestamps and resolver — without copying the reported media into the audit event.

DigiStayBook — internal5.4 · Takedown state model

5.4 · Content moderation / anti-abuse circuit breakers

Stopping report-driven censorship of a wall

All reports go through a server-controlled endpoint with Firebase App Check, transaction-safe post and report updates, same-session and same-post deduplication for 24 hours, and short-lived pseudonymous rate-limit keys.

Reporter breaker

A reporter enters a 24-hour suspected-abuse cooldown after reporting 3 distinct posts on one wall within 10 minutes, or 5 distinct posts within 24 hours.

Wall-level breaker

Trips when 5 distinct posts are reported within 10 minutes across related reporter signals. It pauses new report-driven hides for that wall, alerts Privacy & Safety Operations, and leaves already-hidden posts in review. An internal reviewer can still restrict a credible urgent safety report while the breaker is active.

Confirmed malicious reporting

Two internally confirmed cases within 90 days may create a proportionate restriction lasting no more than 90 days, linked only to a keyed hash of the existing Firebase anonymous or account UID.

No new identity is ever created

No cross-device identity is created, and a cleared browser session cannot be correlated through an invented device fingerprint. Restrictions expire automatically.

Billing operations

Stripe-hosted checkout collects payment details. The product never handles raw card data.

Entitlements are granted or changed only after a signature-verified webhook is processed. Nothing in the browser grants entitlement.

6.4.4–6.4.6 · Typography, interface style and voice

System-first type, tight corners, fixed terminology

Display Aa

--font-display: Iowan Old Style → Palatino → Georgia → serif. A book face, chosen for the guestbook premise. Major brand statements and the wall's editorial moments.

Interface & body Aa

--font-sans: Inter → system UI. All interface text, forms, buttons, tables, emails and guidance.

Scale & measure

Seven fluid steps from 0.72rem to a clamped 2.5–4.25rem, each interpolating with viewport width. One scale across desktop and mobile; content constrained to a 1180px measure. Heading levels are chosen semantically, never for appearance.

Both stacks are system-first, so nothing is downloaded: no font-loading flash, no licensing question, no performance cost. Confirm C-27 — but the display serif resolves differently on macOS, Windows and Android.

Corner radius

2px, 3px, 6px, 10px and a pill token. Deliberately tight — paper does not have 24px corners. Do not introduce new radii per component.

Elevation & texture

Three shadow tokens, reserved for elements that represent physical objects. Depth is not decoration; a flat panel stays flat. A ruled-paper repeating gradient is used sparingly and must never reduce text contrast.

Focus

A 3px ring in --stamp-050 against the --stamp-600 focus colour. Focus is always visible. It is never removed to tidy a layout.

Icons & motion

Inline SVG drawn to the brand, no icon library. Unfamiliar icons carry a visible label — report, pin, hide and delete must never rely on an icon alone. Motion explains change or progress only, and respects reduced-motion preferences.

Pending and held states

"Memory added! Your host is reviewing this post to add it to the public wall." Never suggest the guest did something wrong, and never reveal an internal escalation.

Report acknowledgement — identical every time

"Report received. It will be reviewed." Never confirm deletion and never reveal whether visibility changed.

Billing, privacy and safety

Precise and non-promotional. Defined terms used consistently. No material condition hidden in friendly language. No promotional block in a transactional message.

Fixed terminology

Host, Guest, property, Guest Wall, memory, post, pinned post, QR kit, Privacy & Safety.
Not: user, customer wall, event, gallery, feed.

8.2 · Monthly financial review / once a month, the owner

What the monthly review actually checks

Reconciles Stripe receipts, refunds, disputes and subscription states against the product database.

Reviews supplier invoices and usage against the four drivers — in particular gigabytes stored, moderation calls and emails sent.

Calculates revenue and direct contribution margin per active property.

Identifies unexpected cost growth and its cause — a single heavy property, a retry loop, an unexpected traffic source.

Reviews trial-to-paid conversion, monthly-to-annual mix, cancellations and the reasons given.

Records the next pricing, limit or supplier decision, with an owner and a date.

And confirms the operational commitments with a financial consequence were met

  • The deletion jobs ran
  • The moderation queue was swept daily
  • The monthly false-positive sample of 50 posts was taken
  • No privacy request passed 14 days

Confirm C-02  This review has nothing to review against

Until a target number of paying properties, a trial-to-paid conversion rate and a date are set, the review can describe what happened but cannot say whether it was good enough to keep investing.
