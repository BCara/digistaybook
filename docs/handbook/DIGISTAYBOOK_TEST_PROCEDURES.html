0 Before you start

Two minutes of reading, then you can begin.

There are two sites, and they are not the same

Every check says which site it belongs to. Under Which checks on the left, press Test site or Live site to show only the ones you have been asked to do.

SiteWhat it is
Test siteA practice copy of DigiStayBook. Nothing on it is real — no real money, no real guests, no real photos. You can break things here, and you are meant to. 87 checks.
Both sitesChecks that are done on the test site first, and then repeated on the real site after release.
Live siteThe real DigiStayBook that customers use. Only 34 checks are safe to do here, and only the ones marked for it. 2 of them can only be done here.

If you are testing the live site

  • Only do checks marked Both sites or Live site. The others delete things, break payments on purpose, or send email. On the real site that would affect real customers.
  • Use only the account and the property you were given for testing. Never open, edit or delete anything belonging to a real customer.
  • Never type card details. You will be given a code that makes the payment free. If you are ever asked for a real card, stop and say so.
  • If something fails here, stop and report it straight away. Do not carry on down the list.

Recording what you find

At the bottom of every check there are four buttons and a box for a note.

ButtonUse it when
PassEverything happened the way the “What should happen” column said it would.
FailSomething was different, missing, or went wrong. Stop that check there — do not try to help it along — and write what you saw in the note box.
Not built yetThe thing being checked does not exist in the product yet. The heading usually tells you.
Doesn’t applyYou could not do this one — no phone to hand, no access, or it is a live-site check and you are on the test site. Say which in the note.

Notes are the most useful thing you can give us. “Fail” on its own is hard to act on; “Fail — the email arrived but the date said 1 January 1970” is fixed the same day. A screenshot beats a description.

Your ticks and notes are saved in this browser only. They are not sent anywhere automatically, and they are lost if you clear your browser or open a fresh copy of this file. Press Copy results before you close it.

13.2 · Launch gate

Launch may not be signed off until all of the following hold

  1. Every P1 procedure has been executed and passed — not merely reviewed. A Blocked P1 is not a pass.
  2. The five mandatory suppression tests exist as automated tests (TP-EM-03 to TP-EM-07) and run on every change, per the plan's explicit requirement.
  3. The legal review gate C-19 is recorded with versions and date. Every procedure marked Needs legal sign-off stays open until it is — regardless of whether the software behaves correctly.
  4. The cancellation template is disabled until its dormancy wording is approved (TP-EM-15).
  5. No marketing sequence is enabled while A-06 is outstanding.
  6. Launch ownership is assigned — who approves the launch, against which evidence, and who may stop it. C-31
