# QR placard Pins: drafts for review

Prepared October 30, 2026. Six Pinterest Pins (1000 × 1500) showing the printed QR placard the app makes today, staged
in different places a host would put it. Nothing is scheduled or posted; each Pin needs its own approval (Cara, Joe, or both).

- Every Pin argues one reason from "Why choose DigiStayBook" (`MARKETING_NOTES.md`), noted per Pin.
- No currency, no booking-platform names, no promise of fewer messages, reviews or bookings.
- Pinterest is about twice a week; suggested slots are US evenings, 8:00 pm Eastern (12:00 pm Sydney the next day).
- **Before posting:** each short link below needs a redirect in `firebase.json` (none added yet), and the site deployed.
- The card's main code scans to the demo wall, `digistaybook.com/stay/demo-cottage`. The Wi-Fi code is the demo
  network (SEABREEZE-5G), which doesn't exist, so a phone that scans it just fails to join. That's harmless, but
  if it matters, re-run the build with the Wi-Fi block removed.

Board suggestions: **Vacation Rental Host Tips** for 03 and 04, **Guest Welcome Ideas** for 01, 02 and 05, and
**Vacation Rental Guestbook** for 06. Board names stay free of booking-platform names.
