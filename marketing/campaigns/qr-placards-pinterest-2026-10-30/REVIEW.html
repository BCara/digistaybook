QR placard styles

Eighteen looks for the same 5 × 7 card, all filled with the demo property. Twelve are illustrated and six use photographs of the place. Each has the welcome, the property name, the guest-wall code, the invite, the address, the Wi-Fi code and the DigiStayBook mark. Both codes scan on every style.

Files and printing

Print files are 1500 × 2100 at 300 dpi (5 × 7 in), with no bleed, so print with a small white margin or at "fit". Pinterest versions are 1000 × 1500 with the card on a matching backdrop and no ad copy.

Every code is dark ink on white with its full quiet zone. That holds on the dark styles too (cabin, chalkboard), where the code sits on its own white panel.

The illustrated styles use free Google Fonts (SIL Open Font License) and hand-drawn shapes. The photo styles use the demo property's own Unsplash photos (Unsplash License, credited in the project). No AI imagery anywhere.

The photo styles use the full-size originals (3200 px), so they print sharp at 5 × 7.

Anyone can make these with their own name, link, Wi-Fi and photo on the free sign maker at digistaybook.com/qr-sign-maker (live after the next deploy). Each Pin links there with its style open; titles, descriptions, alt text and dates are in PINS.md.
