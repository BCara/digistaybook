## Sun 8 Nov — Your first welcome, in three lines

Files: fb-05-worksheet.png

Try this before you add another house rule.

Write three short lines:
1. “Welcome to…” — the feeling you want guests to have.
2. “One thing we love about this home…” — a detail only you could share.
3. “Please help us look after…” — one clear request, with a reason.

For example:
“Welcome to our little garden cottage. We planted the herb beds during our first winter here. Please snip a few leaves for dinner and leave the roots growing for the next guests.”

That is example copy, not a customer story. Keep the welcome brief, then put practical instructions in your house essentials and host notes.

# Suggested replies

## “Does it stop guests damaging a home?”

No guest guide can guarantee that. DigiStayBook gives your welcome and practical instructions a clear place to live, so guests can understand how you would like the home looked after.

## “Can I control my reviews?”

You control the welcome, guidance and memories shown on your DigiStayBook wall, including an option to approve new memories. Reviews on booking platforms stay on those platforms.

## “Do guests need another app?”

No. Guests open DigiStayBook in their browser from the property QR display. They do not need a guest account.

## “Can people leave private feedback?”

Yes. There is a separate private-feedback form for the host. It does not replace messages or reviews through the booking platform.

## “Do I have to approve every memory?”

You can switch on approval for new memories. When it is off, clear memories can publish after safety checks.

## A host shares a blunt house rule

Thanks for the example. You could try: “[clear request], because [true reason]. You will find [practical help] at [actual location].” Keep the boundary and make it easy to follow.

## A host shares an upsetting guest incident

That sounds frustrating. Please avoid posting guest names or identifying details here. What one instruction would you want the next guest to find more easily?

## Someone asks for pricing

The current plans are shown on the DigiStayBook pricing page. Check the live page before quoting a price or trial offer. No price or limited offer is part of this campaign.
