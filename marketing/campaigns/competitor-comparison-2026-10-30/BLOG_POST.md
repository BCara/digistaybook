# Digital guidebooks, guest journals or a paper binder? An honest comparison for vacation rental hosts

Most vacation rentals greet guests in one of three ways: a digital guidebook sent as a link, a paper binder and guestbook on the counter, or nothing much at all. A newer kind of tool, the digital guest journal, adds a fourth option.

We make DigiStayBook, so we're not neutral. What we can do is be specific. Below we compare DigiStayBook with three tools hosts often weigh up (Touch Stay, Hostfully Digital Guidebooks and StayPage) plus the paper binder, using only what each company says on its own website as of October 30, 2026. Where another tool does something better, we say so.

## What each option is for

The tools fall into two camps.

**Guidebooks inform.** Touch Stay and Hostfully Digital Guidebooks are built to get house information and local recommendations in front of guests: Wi-Fi, check-in, appliances, places to eat. They are mature, well-featured products.

**Guest journals collect.** StayPage describes itself as a "digital guest journal." Guests leave stories, photos and local finds. Its own FAQ is clear that it isn't a guidebook.

**DigiStayBook does both, from one QR code.** Guests scan a placard on the counter and open a page in their browser. House guidance (Wi-Fi, trash day, checkout) and the host's local favorites sit at the top. Below that is a guest wall of photos and notes left by earlier guests, so the place builds up a story over time. The paper binder sits in the same spot, but it can't be updated from your phone and eventually fills up.

## Touch Stay

Touch Stay is a dedicated digital guidebook with a lot of reach. Its homepage says guidebooks are "mobile-first, with no app required" and can be shared by direct link, QR code or automated guest messaging through a property management system. It lists auto-translation into 30+ languages and 40+ integrations with property management systems, channel managers and hospitality tools. It also includes a store for selling extras to guests, and a way to capture private guest feedback.

**Where Touch Stay is stronger:** translation, integrations and upselling. If you host many international guests, or you want your guidebook sent automatically from your property management system, Touch Stay covers ground DigiStayBook doesn't. It also has a free plan (limited to 20 guest views per guidebook per month, according to its homepage) and a 14-day free trial.

**What's different:** Touch Stay's pages we checked are about the host informing the guest. We didn't see a place for guests to leave photos or notes for the next guest.

## Hostfully Digital Guidebooks

Hostfully is best known for its property management software, and its digital guidebooks plug into it. Hostfully says your first guidebook is free, with "no credit cards." Its guidebooks offer auto-translation into 16+ languages, can pull recommendations and maps from Google, and can be shared as a link, a downloadable PDF, or a QR code from the share dialog. A Guidebook Marketplace lets hosts offer paid extras such as early check-in or late checkout.

**Where Hostfully is stronger:** if you already run your rentals on Hostfully's software, a guidebook that lives in the same system is hard to beat. The free first guidebook is a low-risk way to try a digital guide, and pulling in Google recommendations saves typing.

**What's different:** the content on Hostfully's guidebook pages comes from the host. We didn't see a way for guests to contribute their own photos or notes.

## StayPage

StayPage is the closest to the guestbook side of DigiStayBook. It describes itself as a digital guest journal: guests scan a QR card and leave stories, photos and local finds, with "no app, account, or password." Hosts can publish pages instantly or hold each one for approval, and hide any page. Its FAQ says it isn't a guidebook.

**Where StayPage is stronger:** if you only want a guest journal and already have your house information covered elsewhere, StayPage is focused on exactly that job. It has a free Starter tier and a 14-day free trial of its Host plan.

**What's different:** you'd need a separate guidebook (or a binder) for Wi-Fi, checkout and the rest. DigiStayBook puts the practical answers and the guestbook behind the same QR code, so guests who open it for the Wi-Fi also see what earlier guests loved.

## The paper binder and guestbook

Plenty of good rentals still run on a binder and a guestbook, and they have real charm. A handwritten note has a warmth no screen fully matches. Paper needs no Wi-Fi, no battery and no setup.

The trade-offs are familiar. When the Wi-Fi password or trash day changes, every page has to be reprinted. A paper guestbook fills up, gets coffee on it, or walks off in a suitcase, and the next guest can only read what's on the shelf. And there's no simple way to say something to the host privately while the stay is still underway.

Doing nothing is a choice too. Guests will find their own way, but the stay can feel more like a transaction than a visit.

## Where DigiStayBook fits

DigiStayBook is built around one idea: a rental should feel like staying in someone's home, not a business. In practice that means:

- **Guests can find answers themselves.** Wi-Fi, trash day, appliance quirks and checkout steps are pinned at the top of the wall, in the browser, with no app and no guest account. The QR placard can also carry a Wi-Fi code that a phone joins with one scan.
- **A guestbook that builds up.** Each stay can add photos, notes and local tips for the next guest, alongside your own welcome and favorites.
- **You stay in control of what's shown.** Posts pass automated screening, borderline ones wait for your approval, and you can hide or delete anything. Screening reduces risk; it can't promise every post will be perfect.
- **Hear about problems while you can fix them.** Guests can send you private feedback that's never published, so you hear about the slow shower during the stay.
- **Update once, every guest sees it.** Change the Wi-Fi password on your phone and the next guest sees the new one.
- **Share the memories.** A separate public page shows guest photos and notes (no house details) that you can share with past guests or embed on your own website.
- **One price per property, everything included**, with a 28-day free trial on your first property. [See pricing](/pricing).

A note on house information: booking platforms are changing what hosts may ask of guests. A guest welcome is a place for helpful information, not new rules or chores. Keep your house rules where your booking platform asks you to put them, and keep your welcome friendly.

## Who each option suits best

- **Touch Stay:** hosts and managers who want a full-featured guidebook with translation, integrations and upsells.
- **Hostfully Digital Guidebooks:** hosts already on Hostfully's property management software, or anyone who wants to try one free guidebook.
- **StayPage:** hosts who want a guest journal only and have their house information handled elsewhere.
- **Paper binder and guestbook:** hosts who value handwriting, rarely change their details, and don't mind reprinting.
- **DigiStayBook:** independent hosts and small portfolios who want practical answers and a growing guestbook behind one QR code, with moderation and private feedback included.

Larger operators may also look at all-in-one guest platforms. Enso Connect, for example, describes itself as a guest experience platform for vacation rental brands from 20 listings up, combining a no-download guest web app with check-in, ID verification, upsells, guidebooks and AI messaging.

## When DigiStayBook isn't the right fit

We'd rather you choose the right tool than the wrong one. DigiStayBook probably isn't for you if:

- **You need your guide in several languages.** We don't offer translation.
- **You want the guide sent automatically from your property management system.** We don't integrate with property management systems.
- **You want to sell upgrades or extras to guests.** We don't have an upsell store.
- **You need ID verification, deposits or smart-lock access codes.** Those belong to a different kind of platform.
- **You don't want guest posts at all.** The guest wall is at the heart of DigiStayBook, so if a guestbook isn't part of your plan, a plain guidebook may suit you better.

## FAQ

**Do guests need to download an app or create an account?**
No. Guests scan the QR placard and DigiStayBook opens in their phone's browser. There's no guest account, and DigiStayBook doesn't collect guests' contact details.

**Can I use DigiStayBook alongside another guidebook?**
Yes. Some hosts may prefer to keep an existing guidebook and add DigiStayBook for the guest wall and private feedback. Just keep the information in both consistent.

**What stops someone posting something inappropriate?**
Photos and notes pass automated screening before they're published. Anything borderline waits for your approval, and you can hide or delete any post from your dashboard. No screening system is perfect, which is why you have the final say.

**Is the guest feedback public?**
No. Private feedback goes only to you and is never published on the wall. It isn't a review and doesn't replace one.

**Can I put house rules on the wall?**
The wall is for helpful information, like where the trash cans are and when pickup happens. House rules belong where your booking platform asks you to disclose them before booking.
