## 01-arrival — Bags down. Vacation on.

File: assets/01-arrival.png

Bags down. Shoes off. Now, where’s the Wi-Fi?

The first few minutes in a vacation rental come with little questions. Put your welcome, house essentials and local tips together in DigiStayBook, so guests have somewhere to look when they arrive.

One property QR display opens the guest page in their browser. No guest app or account needed.

## 04-rainy — Rain outside. A good stay inside.

File: assets/04-rainy.png

A rainy afternoon can still turn into a good vacation memory.

If your place has board games, books or a favorite rainy-day outing nearby, tell guests where to find them. A small note can make the things you’ve thoughtfully provided easier to enjoy.

DigiStayBook brings your house guidance and personal tips into one guest page.
